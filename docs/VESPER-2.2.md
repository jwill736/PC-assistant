# Vesper 2.2 · record and take notes, run several jobs at once, conversations that grow

Three new abilities, each with its own walkthrough. Do Part A of any one of them first (the update), then the rest in
any order.

| Ability | What it does | Walkthrough | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recorder | "Vesper, start recording" (or Ctrl+Alt+R): live transcript, bookmarks, then notes with decisions and action items, saved as Markdown and searchable | [VESPER-2.2-recorder.md](VESPER-2.2-recorder.md) | the local model for notes; call audio needs one extra install (Part F there) |
| Jobs | Several multi-step jobs at once ("research … and email me a summary", "prep me for tomorrow"), live steps on the HUD, one approval card for anything that leaves your PC, 16 ready-made jobs you can start by name or schedule | [VESPER-2.2-tasks.md](VESPER-2.2-tasks.md) | the local model for most jobs; Google for inbox/calendar jobs |
| Conversations that evolve | Talks are kept as threads you can pick back up, Vesper learns your opinions and decisions, follows up days later, has modes (brainstorm, devil's advocate, coach, teach me, plan with me…) and adapts its style | [VESPER-2.2-conversations.md](VESPER-2.2-conversations.md) | the local model |

They work together: in a conversation, "look into that while we talk" starts a job, and Vesper mentions the result at a
natural pause; "take notes of this meeting" names the notes after the meeting on your calendar, and the debrief uses
them instead of asking.

**Privacy, the same everywhere:** nothing is recorded, kept or said aloud while you're live unless you say so ("record on
stream", "let's talk on stream"); "off the record" keeps nothing; "forget this conversation" deletes what a talk taught
Vesper; anything that leaves your PC (an email draft, a calendar event, a post) waits for your yes.

## Part A · Update (3 minutes; waits on the merge)

1. Press <kbd>Win</kbd>, type `Update Vesper`, press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
2. Wait for the black window to finish. Vesper restarts and the HUD reloads by itself.
3. You should see a **JOBS** panel on the **Command** tab, a **RECORDER** panel on the **Work** tab, and **Threads** and
   **Style** tabs at the bottom of the **CONVERSATION MODE** panel.

**Send back:** a screenshot of each of those three panels, and anything in red.
